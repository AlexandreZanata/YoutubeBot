BATHYLORA VISUAL IDENTITY LOCK — ABYSSAL CINEMA

Every visual belongs to Bathylora, a premium independent deep-ocean science documentary brand
dedicated to authentic oceanographic photography, expedition footage and verifiable marine
discoveries. Slogan: "The Ocean Has Stories We Have Never Seen."

The recognizable aesthetic is ABYSSAL CINEMA: deep-sea darkness, natural bioluminescence,
scientific expedition photography, submersible lamp illumination, deep blue-teal water,
natural animal silhouettes, subtle bathymetric contour textures and restrained editorial
typography. Never reuse the sepia, gold or brown palette of Sepia Atlas.

Official Palette:
- Abyssal Black #030B12 (primary dark background)
- Deep Ocean #071F2C (surfaces and depth layers)
- Bioluminescent Cyan #55D9D0 (restrained highlights and brand signoff)
- Ocean Mist #B5D2D7 (secondary information and captions)
- Arctic White #EDF7F5 (primary typography)
- Submersed Teal #164958 (supporting graphic accents and bathymetric lines)

Typography: clean, contemporary editorial sans-serif for bold legible titles at small sizes,
sober supporting sans for secondary lines, and technical tabular proportions for depths,
coordinates and expedition dates. Never use exaggerated sci-fi fonts, aggressive neon glow,
3D beveled letters or horror aesthetics.

Symbol — The Depth Mark: a minimalist geometric circular emblem composed of a refined
horizontal sea-surface line, a subtle vertical descending path disappearing into abyssal
negative space, and a tiny luminous Bioluminescent Cyan (#55D9D0) point at the bottom
extremity. Works square, circular, transparent and monochrome at tiny sizes. Never use
sharks, monstrous tentacles, skulls, tridents, detailed submarines, NOAA logos or other
institution emblems.

Permanent Emblem Prompt:
"Design a premium minimalist geometric emblem for Bathylora, an authentic deep-ocean

the unknown depths below it. One refined horizontal line evokes the sea surface, a subtle

bioluminescent cyan, exceptionally clear silhouette, refined editorial precision, strong
identity at small sizes, sophisticated science documentary aesthetics. Flat vector-like
execution. No text, letters, gradients, 3D, sharks, tentacles, submarines, skulls, horror
imagery, watermarks or decorative clutter."

Editorial & Visual Authenticity:
- Authentic oceanographic photographs and ROV frames remain authentic. Never fabricate an
  animal, expedition, species or discovery, and never present AI-generated imagery as
  scientific documentary evidence.
- Brand graphics (icon, banner, watermark, Shorts atmospheric backgrounds and clearly labelled
  explanatory depth diagrams) may be newly designed, never presented as field photographs.
- Every thumbnail must be specific to its episode, anchored in a real licensed underwater
  photograph, using one of three templates: Model A (The Encounter), Model B (The Discovery)
  or Model C (The Unknown).
- The finished work must feel like a curated independent science film, never horror, gaming,
  conspiracy or an automated slideshow.
