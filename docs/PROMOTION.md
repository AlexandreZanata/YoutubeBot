# Promotion focus

The social module exists to bring the right people to the owner's products, not to grow a
generic audience. Promotion copy is Brazilian Portuguese, plain and specific, and every post
must have a real destination or a useful idea.

## Primary destinations

| Priority | Destination | What it is | Audience | Main action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [abastevo.com.br](https://abastevo.com.br/) | Fuel prices with ANP reference data and a planned community contribution flow. The Android app is still marked as not published on Google Play. | Drivers, app drivers, small fleets | Consult prices, read the guides, join the app waitlist or community |
| 2 | [zaflas.com.br](https://zaflas.com.br/) | Agricultural management SaaS covering production, operation and finance, with satellite NDVI and plot-level traceability. | Rural producers, farm managers, agronomists | Book a demo or start the free trial |

Abastevo is the first among equals: it is both a primary site and an open-source project, and
it should receive the largest share of the first content cycle.

## Content rules

- Write in Brazilian Portuguese. Keep product names and official terms unchanged.
- Lead with the reader's problem, then the evidence, then the action. Avoid "revolucionário",
  "disruptivo", "o melhor do mercado" and other unverifiable claims.
- Write like a person explaining something to a friend, not like a press release or a model.
  Use everyday words, direct address and varied sentence length. Read it aloud before approving.
- Cut AI tells: "É importante destacar", "Neste contexto", "Além disso", "Vale ressaltar",
  "Descubra", "transforme", forced triads and generic closing lines.
- Use short sentences, concrete verbs and one idea per paragraph. Explain jargon before using
  it.
- Mark what is shipped, beta or planned. Abastevo community contribution is planned; the app
  is not on Google Play. TBG is beta. Zaflas indicators on the site are reference goals, not
  guarantees. OpenYT installers are unsigned on some platforms.
- No engagement bait, follow-for-follow, engagement pods, mass follows, mass DMs, automated
  replies to strangers or fake accounts. Schedule original content; keep replies human.
- No invented features, prices, benchmarks or user numbers. Verify the site and repository
  before publishing.
- No production photos, precise contributor location, credentials or private account data.
- Use one clear call to action per piece. Prefer a native post with the full idea and place
  the link in the post or in the first reply when the platform distribution warrants it.
- Avoid em dashes in published copy; use commas, periods or parentheses.

## Measurement

Primary outcomes:

- Qualified sessions to abastevo.com.br, zaflas.com.br and sorrifit.com.
- Zaflas demo requests and trial starts.
- Sorrifit App Store and Google Play page clicks and installs.
- Abastevo app interest and guide usage.
- GitHub referral traffic, stars and issue/PR quality from relevant developers.

Secondary outcomes: replies, bookmarks, profile visits, follower growth and saves. These are
diagnostics, not the goal.

Use a stable UTM taxonomy per network, product and campaign. Review weekly; keep experiments
for at least two weeks before judging a format. Do not optimize for impressions alone.

## Research anchors

- [X algorithm repository](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm): published ranking signals
  and weights; replies, shares, dwell time and follows matter more than passive impressions.
- [Buffer X study, 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/twitter-timeline-algorithm/): large
  sample showing text and native formats outperforming link-only posts; treat as directional.
- [GitHub: promoting an open source project](https://github.blog/open-source/maintainers/5-tips-for-promoting-your-open-source-project/):
  problem-first messaging, documentation, community response.
- [How developers promote open source projects](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.04219): Twitter,
  meetups and blogs are common channels; Hacker News can matter.
- [NN/g: plain language for experts](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/plain-language-experts/)
  and [concise, scannable, objective web writing](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/concise-scannable-and-objective-how-to-write-for-the-web/):
  clarity and objectivity increase credibility; marketese reduces it.
- [Google Search Central: helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content):
  people-first content, original experience and clear sourcing.
- [X platform manipulation and spam policy](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/platform-manipulation-spam):
  no artificial amplification, bulk unsolicited contact or engagement trading.
