## Title patterns

The catalog repeatedly uses four hooks:

1. **A Prayer for...**: 16 videos. Example: *A Prayer for When You Feel Invisible*.
2. **The Story of...**: 13 videos. Example: *The Story of Ruth: Loyalty That Changed a Future*.
3. **When You...**: 10 videos. Example: *When You Feel Like Giving Up, God Is Still With You*.
4. **When God...**: 5 videos. Example: *When God Says Wait, He Is Still Working*.

Other titles name a biblical character and a promise or conflict, such as *Zacchaeus: When
Jesus Sees the Person Others Overlook* or *Why God Sometimes Makes You Wait*.

Titles are specific, compassionate and honest. They avoid numbers, all caps, shock, and
promises of wealth or instant breakthrough.

## Content structure

The descriptions show a consistent architecture:

1. **Relatable emotional opening**: exhaustion, regret, waiting, financial pressure, grief,
   doubt, feeling unseen or afraid of the future.
2. **A specific biblical anchor**: one story, character or passage, such as Peter's
   restoration, Joseph's plans, Zacchaeus in Luke 19, Elijah in the wilderness, David, Paul,
   Psalm 23 or Matthew 11.
3. **Reflective application**: what the story reveals about God's character and the listener's
   inner life.
4. **A prayer or quiet invitation**: bring the burden honestly to God; ask for trust, peace,
   wisdom, repentance or strength.
5. **A hopeful closing**: grace for the next step, not a promise that every circumstance
   changes immediately.

The narration paraphrases Scripture and references it naturally. It does not invent verses,
quote long passages verbatim, or turn the story into a sermon with headings and bullet points.

## Production recipe

1. Choose one concrete emotional problem and one specific biblical story or passage.
2. Open with a vivid, relatable scene or question.
3. Retell the story faithfully, with historical and emotional detail.
4. Connect the story to the listener's inner life without preaching clichés.
5. End with a prayer or quiet invitation and a hopeful next step.
6. Keep the narration at 2,400 to 2,800 words for the normal 15 to 17 minute target.
7. Generate metadata with one title, 100 to 180 word description, 5 to 8 lowercase hashtags
   and 15 to 25 English tags.
8. Generate one Sacred Brown Engraving thumbnail; never attach a fixed face reference.
9. Use the brown stippling background and keep the audio as the focus.
