## Thumbnail formula

Measured from the recent catalog, all 1280x720:

1. Left 45 percent is a solid black panel.
2. Top left: yellow rounded pill with an open-book icon and **BOOK SUMMARY** in black
   ultra condensed uppercase.
3. Title in three or four stacked lines of white ultra condensed heavy uppercase, with one or
   two key words in yellow (`#F2C230`).
4. A thin rule, then the author name in white letterspaced uppercase and one short yellow
   subtitle line with the book's theme.
5. Center: dark cinematic scene tied to the book's idea, usually a figure seen from behind.
6. Right: realistic 3D mockup of the book cover with its title and author printed.
7. Palette: black, white, yellow, muted cinematic browns and grays. No channel logo, no
   watermark, no extra text.
