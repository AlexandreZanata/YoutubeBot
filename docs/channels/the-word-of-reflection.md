## Thumbnail formula

Measured from the recent catalog, all 1280x720:

1. Full-bleed cinematic photograph, no side panel and no badge.
2. One biblical figure in an ancient Near-East setting: woven robes, dust, stone, olive trees,
   a doorway or a hillside at golden hour.
3. Emotional, believable face and hands, warm golden light, deep shadows, earth tones.
4. Two or three words in huge white ultra-bold sans, uppercase, stacked, upper left or center:
   *SILENT PRAYER*, *BREAKING POINT*.
5. No outline, glow, logo, watermark, book cover or extra text.
