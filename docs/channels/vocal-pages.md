## Themes and approach

Reflective essays and narrative parables about psychology, identity, fear, memory, desire,
belonging and meaning, always connected to ordinary modern life. Each episode takes one
concrete image or question and unfolds it slowly through psychology, anthropology, myth and
small human scenes.

Title patterns from the catalog:

- *The River Beneath the City: The Ancient Instincts Still Controlling Modern Life*
- *The Enemy in Your Head: Why Some People Haunt Your Thoughts*
- *The Keeper of the Gate: What If Your Fear Is Guarding the Life You Want?*
- *The Mirror of the Enemy: Why Some People Get Under Your Skin*
- *She Prayed Every Night for Five Months | Then the Silence Changed Everything*

Titles are either a metaphor plus a colon subtitle, or a second-person question. The
description is one topic paragraph of about 150 words, followed by six to eight lowercase
hashtags and a `TAGS:` line.

## Video format

- 1920x1080, static brand card for the whole narration: near-black navy gradient, warm gold
  light from the upper left, faint star arc, floating dust and a thin gold open-book line at
  the bottom center. Serif `Vocal Pages` and the tagline sit in the middle.
- Narration with the generated brand-card loop `assets/backgrounds/vocal_pages.mp4`
  (1920x1080, 24 s, seamless) and burned-in subtitles from the standard render path.
- No music cues in the script. The channel relies on the voice and the stillness of the card.
- The thumbnail is separate from the video card and follows the formula below.

## Thumbnail formula

Measured from the six most recent thumbnails, all 1280x720:

1. Solid black panel occupying the left 38 to 40 percent of the frame, full height.
2. Huge white ultra-condensed uppercase title, stacked in three or four lines, filling the
   panel height, tight leading, no outline and no gradient.
3. Optional subtitle under the title: either gold serif with a thin ornamental divider, or
   white bold sans in two short lines.
4. Right side: cinematic composite illustration of the story, painterly-photoreal, dark with
   warm gold and deep teal accents, one clear central subject and symbolic depth.
5. Short thumbnail title, usually shorter than the YouTube title: *The Unanswered Prayer* for
   *She Prayed Every Night for Five Months*.
6. No logos, no channel name, no borders and no more than one secondary line of text.
