# Bathylora — Episode 1 research plan

Working title: **What NOAA Cameras Really Found in the Deep Ocean**

Status: pilot documentary production package prepared for owner review. Built exclusively on
authentic public-domain / open-license oceanographic photographs (primarily NOAA Office of
Ocean Exploration and Research — `PD-USGov-NOAA` — and USGS). Never published or scheduled
automatically.

## Editorial angle

Rather than a sensational list of "weird monsters", this episode follows how remotely operated
vehicles (ROVs) such as NOAA's *Deep Discoverer* aboard *Okeanos Explorer* carry lights and
high-definition cameras into ocean zones humans could never visit in person — revealing real
ecosystems shaped by crushing pressure, darkness, cold and chemical energy.
