# Midnight Tales

Long-form sleep stories for adults, in English, on `@Midnight1Tales`
(`UCLukC7RYzHlXHjtnlkbe4Vw`). One long video every two days at 21:00.

## Format

- 6,500 to 9,000 words, about 45 to 65 minutes at a calm narration pace.
- Third person, past tense, one place, one gentle mystery or journey and a small cast.
- Soothing atmospheric prose about weather, rooms, objects and lamplight. No horror, violence,
  crime detail, sexual content, sudden loud events or dream twists.
- Soft resolution, no moral and no lesson list. Never address the listener directly.

## Visual identity

- 1280x720 painterly cinematic art, soft grain, deep shadows, one warm light source.
- Muted palette per story: blue, green, amber or brown.
- A small human figure seen from behind or far away, subject in the lower two thirds, calm
  negative space above. No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks.
- The thumbnail is also the still video background (`background = thumbnail`).

## Metadata

Titles are evocative and literary, at most 100 characters, optionally with a subtitle such as
"A Story of Memory and Home". The description is one 120 to 170 word story synopsis followed by
`#fiction #audiostory #sleepstory #literaryfiction #bedtimestory #cozystory` and a `TAGS:` line.

## Shorts

One Short every two days at 09:00, 90 to 130 English words, one quiet self-contained moment,
one generated vertical image in the same art style and no custom thumbnail.
